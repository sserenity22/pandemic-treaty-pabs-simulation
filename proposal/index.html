Since May 2025 the working group has met eight times. The 30 April 2026 on-screen text, the last one published, has almost nothing marked as agreed. WHA79 extended the mandate and added legally binding contracts. IGWG-9 runs 2 to 13 November; WHA80 is the deadline. A special Assembly in December 2026 is possible but not confirmed.

One bargain blocks the annex: is access to pathogens linked to benefits?

Group for Equity · Africa Group · Pakistan

“We cannot accept, under any configuration, an approach that delinks access from benefit sharing”

Indonesia, for the Group for Equity · TWN, 13 Jul 2026

“binding benefit-sharing commitments at the point of use”

Algeria, for the Africa Group · HPW, 17 Jul 2026

EU · Switzerland · IFPMA

“This transactional approach seems to us very unsuited to get to good results”

EU negotiator · HPW, 11 Sep 2026

“linking access to financial obligations, risks creating barriers to rapid sharing”

IFPMA statement, IGWG-6, 23 Mar 2026

Neither side can see what its own position would deliver in an outbreak. Positions are argued in the abstract.

The fault line, in the parties' own words. The South's red line is delinking; the EU and industry call linkage transactional and a barrier. Open versus registered access is the same fight: if users stay anonymous, no obligation can be enforced. Country quotes mostly come via TWN and Health Policy Watch because the sessions are closed.

A coordinator submits a position on all seven at once, because the deal is a trade across issues: give on open access, get the money; give on the PHEIC percentage, get the link. The bridge column is our term sheet proposal, each term drawn from a landing-zone signal already in the room.

One limit we state openly: the service hosting the link can see that it was opened, and by which signed-in account.

Neutrality is the asset. If the Equity Group sees an industry tool, or the EU sees an advocacy tool, it is dead in the room. So the same test runs for every bloc, every assumption is open, and nothing a delegation tests is visible to anyone else, including us.

Built on a static web app (the MVP) plus a Claude Managed Agent for reading text, one isolated session per delegation, hosted by a neutral institution with clear data-residency terms.

The MVP already holds the model and the interface. The prototype adds the one thing a coordinator needs in the room: loading today's text and typing new wording. The agent's job is narrow: map text to the model's parameters, cite the paragraph, and say when wording falls outside what the model covers.

The prototype lands for the 30 November to 4 December session and a possible December special Assembly. The validated version is what delegations can cite. Each step has a gate: coordinators must find the previous step useful before we spend on the next.

Neutral by governance, not just by design

Governance

  • A neutral host institution
  • A review panel drawn from every bloc
  • Method, data and weights published
  • Funded by no party to the negotiation
RiskResponse

The biggest risk is perceived bias, so governance carries as much weight as code. Funding from a party to the debate would end the tool's usefulness; we would seek funders outside the negotiation.
