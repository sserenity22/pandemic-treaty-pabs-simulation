## Calculation provenance for plates 04 and 05

Numbers and monthly series are derived from `evaluate()` using the `baseline` and `bridge` presets in the updated demo's `model.mjs`, version `demo-2.0.0`, at a six-month horizon. Pakistan is a display context. The priority population of 10 million, regimen, prices, timing and coefficients are all invented demonstration assumptions.

- First usable delivery: day 134 versus day 97; 37 days earlier.
- Six-month priority coverage: 20.2% versus 56.7%; +36.5 percentage points.
- Cost per complete two-dose regimen: US$13.00 versus US$9.85.
- Cost for the same 1 million people: US$13.00 million versus US$9.85 million, an arithmetic equal-service comparison.
- Access remains Conditional in both selected packages.

The curve uses the model's monthly series, rather than an independently drawn trend. Costs for a fixed 1 million people are not the model's total expenditure for its different coverage levels. These plates are fictional design demonstrations, not country forecasts or statements of actual treaty entitlements, eligibility, finance, delivery or clinical outcomes.
